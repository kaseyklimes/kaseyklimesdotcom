# Photography ranking

Reviewed September 13, 2026, from the images referenced by the local site's photography content. All 42 original photographs were viewed, including every series image and the two entries hidden from the unfiltered grid. The packing-the-car photograph was subsequently removed at your request; the current ranking contains the 41 remaining photographs. Unreferenced originals and alternative grades are not separate entries. Blog and work imagery is outside this ranking because authorship is not consistently established there. This is not a verification of the live deployment.

This is a subjective ranking of standalone photographic interest. Composition, color, light, tonal quality, timing, and subject separation matter alongside narrative, ambiguity, human observation, and the desire to look again. An exceptional achievement in either visual or conceptual interest can lift an image. Grain and softness are judged by their effect, not penalized automatically. Conceptual readings below are interpretations, not claims about the photographer's intent. Neighboring positions are close calls; broad differences matter more.

Red lettering, flowers, and camera strap connect across the frame; the sharply described counter and soft self-reflection create a photograph about both a place and the act of looking. |

| 5 | [Azores — swimming rocks](../public/images/azores1.jpg) | The best expansive scene: pools and rocks organize many small human incidents without losing the landscape's scale. Restrained turquoise and earth tones work beautifully. It rewards a large view much more than a thumbnail. |

| 10 | [Subway sleeper](../public/images/subway-sleeper.jpg) | Red jacket against teal seating anchors a strong portrait of public vulnerability. The tilted head, moving background, and surrounding soft toys add contrasting kinds of movement and stillness. It holds attention beyond the familiar subway subject. |

| 13 | [Berlin balconies](../public/images/berlin-balconies-regraded.jpg) | A compelling relationship between standardized architecture and individual domestic choices. | 15 | [El Salvador — coffee cherries](../public/images/elsalvador-coffee-regraded.jpg) | The hand breaks a dense field of red with clarity and tactile force. | 16 | [Omaha — yellow vehicle at gas station](../public/images/OmahaGasStationFilm.jpg) | Vehicle, disused-looking building, and weeds form a coherent scene of accumulated time. | 17 | [Coney Island — fisherman](../public/images/coney-island-fisherman.jpg) | Excellent teal and blue continuity, interrupted by the orange rod. The lifted gaze and backward lean give a clear action line. The tightly cropped view provides less surrounding narrative than the higher-ranked Coney Island pictures. |
| 18 | [Ladies — kitchen conversation](../public/images/ladies.jpg) | Warm window light and the raised hand make an ordinary domestic scene feel observed and alive. | 19 | [Coney Island — three women on pier](../public/images/coney-island-pier.jpg) | Patterns, hair, and body positions make a lively trio, with the railing carrying the eye into the setting. | 20 | [Iceland — mountains, 2011](../public/images/iceland-2011-regraded.jpg) | Beautiful ochre and gray strata, directional light, and threatening clouds. | 21 | [Bishop — parked vehicles](../public/images/bishop-vehicles-film-grain-v1.jpg) | Repeated vehicles and shadows create a satisfying diagonal against horizontal mountain and field layers. | 22 | [Vancouver — Jolene in the water](../public/images/Vancouver.jpg) | The solitary figure, ripples, and muted monochrome treatment create a wistful, suspended quality. Softness suits that mood. | 23 | [Barcelona — facade and café](../public/images/barcelona.jpg) | Peach facades, blue windows, balcony shadows, and small café figures make an appealing study of lived architecture. The emerging central figure adds a useful focal point, though the facade still dominates the human activity. |

| 25 | [Cuba — café portrait](../public/images/cuba-portrait.jpg) | Clear eyes, flattering soft light, and a nicely separated background make this one of the strongest straightforward portraits technically. | 26 | [Concrete braces](../public/images/concrete-braces-regraded.jpg) | Confident upward framing reveals repeated structural triangles, with foliage adding a contrasting texture. | 32 | [Azores — cat on path](../public/images/azores-cat.jpg) | A charming small discovery with a useful leading path, lush greens, and good depth. The cat is visible but not strongly separated; tower, grass, and sky divide attention without a particularly decisive secondary relationship. |
| 33 | [Trondheim waterfront](../public/images/trondheim-regraded.jpg) | Attractive repeated gables and a strong red–blue palette make an effective travel image. The viewpoint and subject are familiar, and the pale sky adds little. More visually pleasant than conceptually distinctive. |

| 41 | [Azores — dolphin](../public/images/dolphin.jpg) | Appealing water color and a lucky visible leap, but the dolphin is small and its bright body loses definition. The expansive water and washed-out upper coastline do not create enough structure around the moment. |

The clearest recurring strength is finding human traces within visual order: lettering and reflection, a radio beneath a blackboard, a car against a colored facade, swimmers distributed through rock pools, or a makeshift hockey goal. The strongest images give the viewer a second discovery after the initial visual appeal.

For a tight standalone selection, start with the first 12. For series, lower-ranked images may still earn their place through pacing, context, or personal significance; this ranking does not measure those functions.
